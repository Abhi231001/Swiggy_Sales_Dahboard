# Swiggy Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)
Developed an interactive Excel dashboard to analyze Swiggy sales performance using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, and KPI cards. The dashboard provides insights into monthly, weekly, daily, quarterly, state-wise, and city-wise sales while tracking customer ratings, order volume, and average order value to support data-driven business decisions.

# Key Features
Interactive Month and State slicers
KPI cards for Sales, Orders, AOV, and Ratings
Monthly, Daily, Weekly, and Quarterly trend analysis
State-wise sales map and city performance analysis
Built using Excel Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Conditional Formatting, and Dashboard Design

# Problem statement
Which months, weeks, days, quarters generate the highest sales
Which states and cities contribute the most revenue
Whether order volume, sales, and customer ratings are performing consistently
Areas where business can improve operational and marketing decisions

# Insight
1. Overall business performance
Swiggy maintains a healthy combination of high sales and strong customer satisfaction.

2. Monthly sales trend
January recorded the highest sales.
February showed the lowest monthly revenue.
Sales recovered steadily from March onward.
August matched one of the strongest-performing months.

3. Daily sales pattern
Saturday generates the highest revenue.
Tuesday is the weakest sales day.
Weekend demand is noticeably stronger than weekdays.

4. State-wise performance
Southern and western states appear to contribute more revenue.
Some northeastern states have comparatively lower sales.

5.City-wise sales
A few metropolitan cities contribute a large share of revenue, indicating geographic concentration.

6.Quarterly performance

Q2 is the best-performing quarter.
Q1 and Q2 perform similarly.
Q3 is considerably lower, suggesting either partial data or reduced business activity.

# Suggestions
Investigate why February dropped—seasonality, fewer promotions, or reduced customer demand.
Launch Tuesday discount campaigns to increase mid-week orders.
Increase marketing in low-performing regions.
Expand restaurant partnerships where sales penetration is low.
